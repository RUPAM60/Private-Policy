# KITTY Bot — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 18, 2026

KITTY (“the Bot”) is a Discord music bot. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use KITTY.

## 1. Information that may be processed
Depending on the features you use, KITTY may process:
- Discord user, server, and channel IDs needed to run commands.
- Prefix command text and arguments, such as song names or music URLs, so the Bot can process requests.
- Server/member information needed for server statistics, if that feature is enabled.
- Music requests, queues, player state, and error logs needed to provide and troubleshoot playback.
- Settings or records that the Bot actually saves, such as server preferences or access-control settings.

Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information through Bot commands or support messages.

## 2. How information may be used
Information may be used to process commands, provide music playback and server features, apply permissions and settings, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and respond to support requests.

## 3. Storage and service providers
Information may be processed by Discord, KITTY's hosting provider, and any music/audio service or Lavalink server used for playback. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

The owner must verify and accurately describe which services KITTY actually uses, what information is saved, where it is stored, and who can access it. Do not claim that information is never stored or shared unless that is true.

## 4. Sharing
KITTY does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by services required to operate the Bot, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the Bot and its users. Actual practices may depend on KITTY's configuration.

## 5. Retention and deletion
Information should be kept only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required for legitimate operational or legal reasons. Logs and saved settings may have different retention periods.

For a privacy or data request, contact the KITTY team through its official support channel. The team should review requests and delete eligible data where feasible and legally permitted.

## 6. Security
Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Changes
This policy may be updated as KITTY's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
